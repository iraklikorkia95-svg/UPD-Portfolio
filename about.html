ABOUT

IRAKLI KORKIA

Hello, I am Irakli. Originally from Georgia, I am now based in France thanks to a mix of foreseen and unforeseen circumstances.

As you may have noticed from the photos, I am definitely not a professional photographer. It just happens that most days, when I leave my actual work and take the office badge from around my neck, the first thing that goes back there is a camera strap.

I am lucky enough to live in the beautiful city of Strasbourg and to travel now and then with a camera and two lenses, though usually only one makes it out of the bag. After that, I rely on luck: on people, nature, architecture, and light arranging themselves around me at exactly the same moment my camera is ready.

Most days, I come home with fewer photographs than I thought I would. That is part of the game. I hope you enjoy what you see here, and that it reminds you to appreciate the world around you.

Sometimes the camera is just an excuse to pay attention.
